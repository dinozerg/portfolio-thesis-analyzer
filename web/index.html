Assumptions, data & methodology

Prices are daily closing prices adjusted by Yahoo Finance for splits and dividends, retrieved through yfinance. Each holding starts at $1; the portfolio is the starting-weighted sum of these growth series. Buy and hold with fractional shares: no rebalancing, fees, taxes, or external cash flows.

Volatility is sample standard deviation of daily portfolio returns × √252. Sharpe is mean daily excess return ÷ sample daily volatility × √252. The daily risk-free assumption is (1 + annual rate)^(1/252) − 1. Default 0% is an editable simplifying assumption, not a current Treasury yield. Zero volatility produces N/A. Maximum drawdown is the worst decline from a prior peak, including the initial investment.

Average correlation is the unweighted mean of pairwise daily-return correlations among holdings with positive allocations. It is not an adjustable market assumption. The allocation chart and editable weights show starting allocations; buy-and-hold weights drift over time.

All series use common valid dates. No forward filling. Limited history can shorten the period. Missing interior dates make daily annualization approximate. The latest daily bar may be provisional. Historical returns do not forecast future performance. Random mixes sample 30 US equities, not the full market, and are not recommendations.
